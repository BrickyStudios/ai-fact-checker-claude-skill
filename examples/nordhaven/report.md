# Fact-check report: nordhaven-annual-report.pdf

**7 claims checked, 6 findings.** 2 wrong · 3 inconsistent · 1 misleading · 1 confirmed

Coverage: 13 of 13 number-bearing sentences (100.0%). Every quote below was matched verbatim by script and every calculation recomputed.

## C02 · WRONG · page 1

> operating profit rose 14.8% to EUR 186 million

Operating profit rose from EUR 165 million to EUR 186 million. That is 12.7%, not 14.8%.

**Proposed correction:** operating profit rose 12.7% to EUR 186 million

**Calculation:** `(186-165)/165*100` = 12.7 (stated: 14.8)

- Same document, p. 1: "Operating profit 186 165"

## C05 · WRONG · page 1

> every request about personal data is answered within 60 days, as Article 12 of the GDPR requires

Article 12(3) GDPR sets one month, extendable by two further months only where necessary.

**Proposed correction:** every request about personal data is answered within one month, as Article 12 of the GDPR requires

- [GDPR Article 12](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-12-gdpr/): "without undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt of the request"

## C03 · INCONSISTENT · page 1

The key figures table on the same page gives 3,210 employees at year end. Two digits look swapped.

**Proposed correction:** Our workforce grew to 3,210 colleagues across six countries

- Same document, p. 1: "Employees at year end 3,210 3,040"

## C04 · INCONSISTENT · page 1

> Food waste per tonne produced has fallen every year since 2021

The waste table shows a rise in 2023, from 38 to 39 kg per tonne. Three of the four year-on-year changes were falls. The fall from 41 kg to 33 kg since 2021 is 19.5%, about 20%.

**Proposed correction:** Food waste per tonne produced has fallen by 20% since 2021

- Same document, p. 2: "41 38 39 35 33"

## C07 · INCONSISTENT · page 2

> Group 1,840 1,680

The three segments add up to 1,860, not 1,840. Note 4 explains the gap: EUR 20 million of sales between segments are eliminated, but this table has no eliminations row.

**Proposed correction:** Add a row "Eliminations -20" above the group total

**Calculation:** `910+560+390` = 1860 (stated: 1840)

- Same document, p. 2: "Dairy 910 850"
- Same document, p. 2: "Bakery 560 500"
- Same document, p. 2: "Frozen 390 330"
- Same document, p. 2: "Sales between segments of EUR 20 million are eliminated on consolidation"

## C06 · MISLEADING · page 1

> produce 410 MWh a year, enough electricity for about 12,000 Danish households

12,000 households on 410 MWh leaves 34 kWh per household per year. At Ørsted's 4,000 kWh for a typical single-family house, 410 MWh covers about 103 homes.

**Proposed correction:** produce 410 MWh a year, enough electricity for about 100 Danish households

**Calculation:** `410*1000/4000` = 103 (stated: 12000)

- [Ørsted press release](https://orsted.com/en/media/news/2016/08/sharp-increase-in-entertainment-related-power-consumption) (2016): "in an average single-family house with a typical annual consumption of 4,000kWh"
