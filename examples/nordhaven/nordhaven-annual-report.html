Letter from the CEO

Revenue grew 9.5% to EUR 1,840 million in 2025, and operating profit rose 14.8% to EUR 186 million. Our workforce grew to 3,120 colleagues across six countries.

Food waste per tonne produced has fallen every year since 2021. We kept our promise to customers: every request about personal data is answered within 60 days, as Article 12 of the GDPR requires.

The rooftop solar panels installed at our Aarhus site in 2025 produce 410 MWh a year, enough electricity for about 12,000 Danish households.
